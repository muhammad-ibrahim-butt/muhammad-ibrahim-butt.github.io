The problem

A challenge connects content and financial workflows. Users submit videos, vote, receive results, and manage rewards. The backend needs to track deadlines, participant eligibility, winner selection, wallet history, and moderation while supplying consistent data to multiple clients.

Engineering decisions

Coordinate the challenge lifecycle on the backend

The APIs manage challenge creation, discovery, submissions, likes, and comments. Submission validation checks the challenge state and payment requirements. When a challenge ends, the reward workflow selects the winning submission, calculates the distribution, updates wallet balances, and records the result and balance history.

Tied leading submissions can extend the challenge by one hour. Scheduled commands also maintain monthly ranking history and send notifications when rankings change. Keeping these rules in the backend gives the clients a shared interpretation of challenge state.

Make reward and withdrawal activity traceable

Entry fees, winner rewards, platform earnings, and withdrawals have distinct records. The withdrawal workflow exposes pending, accepted, and rejected states to administrators and notifies users of decisions. Wallet history makes balance changes visible alongside the operational action that produced them.

Support media and community operations

Video and image uploads use S3 storage. Queued Firebase Cloud Messaging notifications cover submissions, followers, results, and withdrawals, with user preferences controlling notification categories. Reporting, blocking, account deletion, and admin moderation give the team controls for operating a public community.

Delivery scope

The Laravel API and admin tools support authentication, profiles, social interactions, challenge lifecycle rules, media, reward workflows, rankings, and moderation. The admin interface brings users, challenges, submissions, withdrawals, and content reports into one operational workspace.

Outcome

The backend supports a product distributed through web and mobile channels. Google Play lists 100K+ downloads as of November 11, 2026. The linked product and store pages provide public evidence of distribution; my contribution described here is the backend implementation.
