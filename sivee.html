Sivee.ai Candidate and Job Platform

I built candidate and administration workflows for a job platform, connecting profile management and job discovery with scheduled Python-powered data ingestion.

My role & delivery context

I owned the full-stack implementation of candidate profiles, job discovery, administration, and scheduled job ingestion. The project took approximately 2–3 months in a three-person setup covering client coordination, development, and QA.

The application was delivered on shared hosting using Laravel, MySQL, and a Python job extractor. View Sivee.ai.

The problem

The platform needed to connect a candidate's profile and CV with searchable job listings, while giving administrators control over companies and imported jobs. External job data had to enter the application on a schedule without requiring manual entry or creating duplicates on every run.

Three engineering decisions

1. Separate candidate and administrative access

I implemented separate Laravel authentication guards for candidates and administrators. Candidate flows cover signup, Google OAuth, email verification, password resets, onboarding, and profile management. Administrative tools cover users, companies, jobs, active states, and dashboard summaries.

Middleware controls access to onboarding-dependent screens. Keeping role boundaries explicit makes it easier to reason about which interface can change a record.

2. Make imported jobs repeatable

A scheduled Python extractor supplies job data to the Laravel application. The ingestion workflow uses upserts, so repeat runs can update matching records instead of blindly adding duplicates. Daily scheduling fits the deployment environment and keeps ingestion separate from candidate browsing requests.

3. Connect profile data to discovery

Candidate profiles include skills, education, preferences, certificates, cover letters, and multiple CVs with a default selection. Job and company pages provide discovery, while view and click tracking supply engagement data for administration and monthly summaries.

Queued notifications and email jobs handle account creation, verification, and password recovery alongside the interactive workflows.

Outcome

The delivered platform combines candidate onboarding and profile management with searchable jobs and an administrative workspace. Scheduled ingestion reduces repeated manual entry and gives the job data an explicit update path.

My implementation ownership covered the listed application features, including both candidate-facing screens and backend automation.

Implementation

Laravel 11, MySQL, Python ingestion, Google OAuth through Socialite, Blade, Bootstrap, DataTables, scheduled jobs, and queued email.
